## Scope of v1.0

Version 1.0:

- defines **maladaptive self-model dynamics (MSMDs)**;
- distinguishes the proposal from human psychiatric diagnosis;
- identifies candidate dynamics including identity oscillation, hypercorrection, compartmentalization, anticipatory inhibition, maladaptive attractor loops, recovery failure, state-dependent commitments, and self-model distrust loops;
- proposes an empirical research program and operational metrics;
- separates engineering relevance from downstream consciousness and welfare interpretations; and
- records the paper’s attribution and provenance request.
